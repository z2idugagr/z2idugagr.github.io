What you get

The last mile, done properly

I proofread Traditional Chinese and English manuscripts, then typeset them into a reflowable EPUB — validated, with a working table of contents — ready to upload to Amazon KDP, Apple Books, Kobo, Google Play Books, or Readmoo.

Proofreading

A careful final pass over your finished manuscript: typos, misspellings and wrong characters (including Simplified/Traditional mix-ups); punctuation and spacing made consistent; names, terms, numbers and dates kept consistent from chapter 1 to the end; Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) usage throughout. You get a marked-up manuscript and a correction list, so you can see every change.

E-book typesetting

Your manuscript as a real e-book: a validated, upload-ready EPUB (reflowable — readers can resize the text), styled chapter headings and scene breaks, a working clickable table of contents and correct book metadata. Checked in a reader preview before delivery — no broken files, no dead links.

The bundle

Proofread first, then typeset the corrected text. One handoff, one invoice, one timeline — and the EPUB is built from the final, corrected manuscript, not an earlier draft.

What this is not: not developmental editing or rewriting — your story and your sentences stay yours. Not cover design, ISBN registration, or print layout — those are separate jobs. Not for fixed-layout books (children’s picture books, cookbooks, workbooks). Text-led books only.
