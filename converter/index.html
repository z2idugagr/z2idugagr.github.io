Mainland → Taiwan Wording Converter｜簡轉台繁（台灣用詞）免費轉換器

Paste Chinese copy written with Mainland wording — Simplified or Traditional characters both work — and this free tool converts it in two passes: Simplified characters become Traditional, then Mainland terms are swapped for the words people in Taiwan actually use: 軟件 → 軟體、視頻 → 影片、網絡 → 網路. Every swap is listed below the result, so you can review each one. Conversion runs locally in your browser; your text is never uploaded.

Honest limits 先講清楚： this tool now does two passes — (1) character-level Simplified→Traditional conversion (2,740 standard character mappings) and (2) a Taiwan wording swap against a 141-term dictionary. It is still not a translation and not a human review: characters with more than one Traditional form follow the standard mapping, and the wording pass then fixes common phrases （头发 converts character-by-character to 頭發， and the wording pass corrects it to 頭髮）, sentence structure and tone stay untouched, and terms outside the dictionary keep their Mainland wording. Wording is only the first pass: publish-ready copy still needs a human localization review. 這是字級＋詞彙層的轉換助手，改不到的語感與句構，仍需真人在地化。
