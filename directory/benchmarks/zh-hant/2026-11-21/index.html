Traditional Chinese Quality Benchmark v1.0 · Corpus v1.0 (24 segments) · Tested 2026-11-21 00:48–00:50 CST, browser-route supplement 00:52–00:57 CST · Zero-budget run: free public endpoints and free API tiers only; no new accounts registered.

Conditions disclosure

AI/LLM engines received the corpus with one shared neutral prompt ('Translate into Traditional Chinese as used in Taiwan' plus fixed formatting instructions). Machine-translation engines received the source text directly, with no prompt. The groups were measured under different conditions; this disclosure travels with the scores wherever they appear.

Supplement & Bing condition

Supplement (same run, 2026-11-21 00:52-00:57 CST): DeepL and Microsoft Translator (Bing) were measured afterwards through a real-browser route - free web translators, not logged in - after their scripted channels proved unreachable; the failed attempts remain archived. Bing condition disclosure: the translator's tone preset was left at its default 'casual' setting, so its output uses the informal address form throughout with colloquial phrasing. The benchmark's three metrics (script residue, mainland wording, terminology) do not score register or tone; read Bing's score with that condition attached.
