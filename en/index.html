▍FAQ

Can't I just convert Simplified Chinese to Traditional characters automatically?

Conversion tools change the glyphs, not the wording. Words like 軟件, 視頻 and 網絡 are perfectly valid Traditional characters — and instantly read as "not from here" in Taiwan. Localization replaces the wording, phrasing and tone with what people in Taiwan actually use.
