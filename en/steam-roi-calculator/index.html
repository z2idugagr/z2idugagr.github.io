▍Why Traditional Chinese — and Why Taiwan Wording

Adding "Chinese" is not one decision but two: which script, and whose wording.

Simplified ≠ Traditional

Many games ship Simplified Chinese only and assume it covers Taiwan. It doesn't — different script, different vocabulary, and Taiwan players can tell at a glance. A missing Traditional Chinese page is a missing storefront.

Conversion tools aren't localization

Auto-converting Simplified characters gives you valid Traditional glyphs with mainland wording — 軟件 instead of 軟體， 視頻 instead of 影片. Players read it as "not made for us" in the first three lines.

The store page is the cheap test

You don't need to localize the whole game to test the market. The store page is a few hundred words, it's where wishlists happen, and it's the standard first step before committing to full in-game localization.
