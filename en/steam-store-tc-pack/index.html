▍FAQ

We already have Simplified Chinese. Isn't that enough?

Simplified and Traditional Chinese differ in far more than character shapes. Taiwan players use different words for everyday gaming terms (影片 vs 視頻、伺服器 vs 服務器、品質 vs 質量…), different punctuation and number formats, and they can tell instantly when a page was machine-converted. A converted page reads as foreign; a localized page reads as made for them.

Can't we just run it through Google Translate / AI?

You can — and players will notice. Machine output keeps Mainland vocabulary and literal phrasing. This pack is a native Taiwan translator reviewing every line against a 42-point Taiwan terminology checklist, which is exactly the part machines get wrong.
